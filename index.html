Explainable AI for Clinical Decision Support System

An end-to-end ML pipeline on the UCI Diabetes dataset (~101,766 hospital encounters) that predicts 30-day patient readmission and explains every prediction to clinicians.

  • Benchmarked Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest and XGBoost; XGBoost reached 86.93% accuracy.
  • Leak-free cross-validation and hyperparameter tuning to avoid overly optimistic estimates.
  • Tuned the decision threshold with F2-score to prioritise recall on high-risk patients.
  • SHAP & LIME explanations at global and per-patient level, deployed in an interactive Streamlit app.
